IntentBridge 不是 Prompt Engineering 的变体，也不是某个工具或框架。它是人机协同的一种思想——核心命题是人的意图如何完整、可靠地转化为AI的执行。这个想法来源于我三年深度使用AI解决真实业务问题的过程中，反复遇到的一个痛点。 IntentBridge is not a variant of Prompt Engineering, nor is it a tool or framework. It is a philosophy of human-AI collaboration — the core proposition is how human intent can be completely and reliably translated into AI execution. This idea emerged from three years of deep AI usage solving real business problems, where I kept running into the same pain point.

§ 1

不是理论推导，是真实痛点 Not a theoretical derivation — real pain

2023年，我开始深度使用AI做数据分析、平台开发、产品设计。很快我发现一个反复出现的问题——每次和AI协作，结果和我的预期之间总有偏差。不是AI不够强，也不是我不会写提示词。问题出在更根本的地方。 In 2023, I started using AI intensively for data analysis, platform development, and product design. I soon noticed a recurring problem — every time I collaborated with AI, there was always a gap between the result and my expectation. It wasn't that AI wasn't powerful enough, nor that I couldn't write prompts. The problem was more fundamental.

我心里想的是A，告诉AI的是A′，AI理解的是A″，最终执行的是A′″′ What I have in mind is A, what I tell AI is A′, what AI understands is A″, what it finally executes is A′″′
意图在传递过程中逐级失真——这就是"意图-执行差距" Intent gets distorted at each step of translation — this is the "Intent-Execution Gap"

这四层偏移，在简单任务里不明显。但当你用AI做复杂的事情——搭平台、写规范、做决策支持——每一层偏移都会被放大，最终结果离你的真实意图越来越远。 These four layers of drift are barely noticeable in simple tasks. But when you use AI for complex work — building platforms, writing specs, supporting decisions — each layer of drift gets amplified, and the final result drifts further and further from your true intent.

所以这不是提示词的问题。提示词工程解决的是"怎么说"，而IntentBridge要解决的是更根本的问题——人机之间如何建立可靠的意图传递机制。 So this is not a prompt problem. Prompt engineering solves "how to say it," while IntentBridge addresses a more fundamental question — how to build a reliable intent transfer mechanism between humans and AI.

§ 2

框架 — 核心组成部分 Framework — Core Components

§ 2.1
意图-执行差距模型 Intent-Execution Gap Model 已审查 ✓ Reviewed ✓
描述人的意图在转化为AI执行的过程中，经历的四个阶段及其对应的失真机制。是整个方法论的基础模型。 Describes the four stages human intent passes through on its way to AI execution, and the distortion mechanisms at each stage. The foundational model of the entire methodology.
§ 2.2
六维质量评估框架 Six-Dimension Quality Assessment Framework 已审查 ✓ Reviewed ✓
从完整性、准确性、一致性、可操作性、可审查性、可演化性六个维度评估AI输出的质量。不是打分，是发现差距。 Evaluates AI output quality across six dimensions: completeness, accuracy, consistency, actionability, auditability, and evolvability. Not about scoring — about discovering gaps.
§ 2.3
活标准双环审查 Living Standard Dual-Loop Review 已审查 ✓ Reviewed ✓
双环机制：内环审查案例（AI输出），外环审查标准本身。标准不是固定的——它在审查实践中持续进化。本站的设计规范就是用这套方法审查的。 A dual-loop mechanism: the inner loop reviews the case (AI output), the outer loop reviews the standard itself. Standards are not fixed — they evolve through review practice. This site's design spec was reviewed using this method.
§ 2.4
人机分工模型 Human-AI Division of Labor Model 已审查 ✓ Reviewed ✓
明确哪些决策必须由人做，哪些可以交给AI。不是简单的"人管方向、AI管执行"——而是根据意图清晰度和后果严重性两个维度来划分。 Clarifies which decisions must be made by humans and which can be delegated to AI. Not simply "humans steer, AI executes" — but divided along two dimensions: intent clarity and consequence severity.
