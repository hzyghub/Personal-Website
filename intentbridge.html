我不会写代码。AFC数据平台、这个网站——所有代码都是我和AI一起完成的。三年来我用AI做数据分析、搭平台、写规范，反复遇到同一个问题：我心里想的和AI最终做出来的，总是有偏差。不是AI不够强，也不是我不会写提示词。问题出在更根本的地方——人和AI之间的意图传递，天然就是有损耗的。IntentBridge就是我从实践中摸索出来的应对方法。还不成熟，但在我的项目里确实管用。 I don't know how to code. The AFC data platform, this website — all the code was built through my collaboration with AI. Over three years of using AI for data analysis, platform development, and writing specs, I kept hitting the same problem: what I have in mind and what AI produces are never quite the same. It's not that AI isn't powerful enough, or that I can't write prompts. The problem is more fundamental — intent transfer between humans and AI inherently loses information. IntentBridge is the approach I've developed from practice to deal with this. It's not mature yet, but it genuinely works in my projects.

§ 1

起因 — 真实痛点 Origin — Real pain points

2022年底ChatGPT发布，我是第一批用户。说实话，之前AI对我来说是另一个世界——API、模型、训练，那是工程师的事。但ChatGPT用对话就能用，门槛突然没了。我先是拿它帮忙处理Excel、写小脚本提效，后来逐步深入到数据分析。很快我发现一个反复出现的问题——每次和AI协作，结果和我的预期之间总有偏差。不是AI不够强，也不是我不会写提示词。问题出在更根本的地方。 ChatGPT launched in late 2022, and I was an early adopter. To be honest, before that AI was a different world to me — APIs, models, training were for engineers. ChatGPT changed that: just have a conversation, and the barrier was gone. I started by using it to help with Excel and writing small scripts for efficiency, then gradually moved deeper into data analysis. I soon noticed a recurring problem — every time I collaborated with AI, there was always a gap between the result and my expectation. It wasn't that AI wasn't powerful enough, nor that I couldn't write prompts. The problem was more fundamental.

我心里想的是A，告诉AI的是A′，AI理解的是A″，最终执行的是A′″′ What I have in mind is A, what I tell AI is A′, what AI understands is A″, what it finally executes is A′″′
意图在传递过程中逐级失真——这就是"意图-执行差距" Intent gets distorted at each step of translation — this is the "Intent-Execution Gap"

这四层偏移，在简单任务里不明显。但当你用AI做复杂的事情——搭平台、写规范、做决策支持——每一层偏移都会被放大，最终结果离你的真实意图越来越远。 These four layers of drift are barely noticeable in simple tasks. But when you use AI for complex work — building platforms, writing specs, supporting decisions — each layer of drift gets amplified, and the final result drifts further and further from your true intent.

所以这不是提示词的问题。提示词工程解决的是"怎么说"，而IntentBridge要解决的是更根本的问题——人机之间如何建立可靠的意图传递机制。 So this is not a prompt problem. Prompt engineering solves "how to say it," while IntentBridge addresses a more fundamental question — how to build a reliable intent transfer mechanism between humans and AI.

§ 2

框架 — 核心组成 Framework — Core Components

核心架构 Core Architecture
§ 2.1
核心架构：两层规格 Core Architecture: Two-Layer Specs 已审查 ✓ Reviewed ✓
AI的执行质量上限，由规格质量决定——协作规格+知识规格，缺一不可。 AI's execution quality ceiling is determined by spec quality — collaboration specs + knowledge specs, both indispensable.
我发现AI干活的质量上限，由你给它的"说明书"质量决定。这份说明书分两层：协作规格告诉AI怎么工作——工作方式、边界、约束（比如我写的那个603行的CLAUDE.md）；知识规格告诉AI做判断需要的领域知识——术语定义、规则集、案例库。在AFC项目里，协作规格就是CLAUDE.md，知识规格就是我整理的100多个地铁专业术语定义和业务逻辑手册。光有协作规格没有知识规格，AI不知道依据什么做判断；光有知识规格没有协作规格，AI不知道怎么用这些知识。两层缺一不可。 I found that AI's execution quality ceiling is determined by the quality of "instructions" you give it. These instructions have two layers: collaboration specs tell AI how to work — methods, boundaries, constraints (like the 603-line CLAUDE.md I wrote); knowledge specs give AI the domain knowledge it needs for judgment — terminology, rule sets, case libraries. In the AFC project, the collaboration spec was the CLAUDE.md, and the knowledge spec was the 100+ metro terminology definitions and business logic manuals I compiled. Collaboration specs without knowledge specs leave AI with no basis for judgment; knowledge specs without collaboration specs leave AI unsure how to use them. Both layers are indispensable.
两个规格层 Two Spec Layers
§ 2.2
协作规格层：六维评估 + 撰写指南 Collaboration Spec Layer: Six-Dim Assessment + Writing Guide 已审查 ✓ Reviewed ✓
六个维度评估协作规格质量（0-12分），配套撰写指南覆盖13项必填内容、7个反模式。 Six dimensions assess collaboration spec quality (0-12 points), with a complete writing guide covering 13 required items and 7 anti-patterns.
我用六个维度来判断一份协作规格写得好不好：AI拿到这份文档能不能直接上手（冷启动充分性）、会不会浪费token（执行成本控制）、里面有没有自相矛盾的规则（规则冲突检测）、过一阵子会不会过时（时间衰减保护）、边界清不清楚（范围边界清晰度）、内容有没有错（内容正确性）。每个维度0-2分，满分12分。我还基于这六个维度写了一份完整的撰写指南（v1.4），覆盖13项必填内容、4步撰写流程、7个常见错误。已适配 Claude Code、Cursor、Copilot、Windsurf 等主流AI编程工具。 I use six dimensions to judge whether a collaboration spec is well-written: can AI get started with just this document (cold start sufficiency), will it waste tokens (execution cost control), are there contradictory rules (rule conflict detection), will it become outdated over time (temporal decay protection), are the boundaries clear (scope boundary clarity), and is the content correct (content correctness). Each dimension scores 0–2, total 12. I also wrote a complete writing guide (v1.4) based on these six dimensions, covering 13 required items, a 4-step writing process, and 7 common mistakes. Adapted for Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot, Windsurf, and other major AI coding tools.
§ 2.3
知识规格层：领域知识显性化 Knowledge Spec Layer: Making Domain Knowledge Explicit 已审查 ✓ Reviewed ✓
面向领域专家——把你脑子里的专业判断，变成AI能理解、能使用的东西。 For domain experts — turning the professional judgment in your head into something AI can understand and use.
这部分是给非技术人员用的。核心问题就一个：你脑子里的专业判断，怎么变成AI能用的东西？我在AFC项目中摸索出四种方法——反例法（从"这是错的"反推规则，比如"二维码OD配对不能用全部记录，因为有人进站后从别的口出"）、追问法（层层追问自己"我凭什么这么判断"）、对比法（找相似案例的关键差异）、边界法（找规则的例外和失效条件）。整理出来的知识放在四种地方：术语表、规则集（IF-THEN）、范例库（好的坏的边界案例都要有）、权威来源清单。五个维度（完整性、准确性、无歧义性、时效性、隐性知识覆盖率，0-10分）来检查质量够不够。 This part is for non-technical people. The core question is simple: how do you turn the professional judgment in your head into something AI can use? I developed four methods from the AFC project — counter-example (infer rules from "this is wrong," e.g., "you can't use all QR records for OD pairing because some people exit from different entrances"), deep questioning (keep asking yourself "why do I judge it this way"), comparative (find the critical difference between similar cases), and boundary (find exceptions and failure conditions). Organized knowledge goes into four places: glossary, rule sets (IF-THEN), example libraries (good/bad/boundary cases), and authoritative source lists. Five dimensions (completeness, accuracy, ambiguity-free, timeliness, tacit-knowledge coverage, 0–10 points) check whether the quality is sufficient.
保障机制 Safeguards
§ 2.4
前置过程：意图触达 Pre-Spec Process: Intent Reaching
规格撰写之前的前置步骤。陈述需求≠真实需求——AI主动探索帮你发现真正的意图。 The pre-spec step. Stated requirements ≠ real requirements — AI proactively explores to uncover your true intent.
写规格之前有一个前置步骤——搞清楚你真正想要什么。很多时候你自己说出来的需求和你心里真正想的不是一回事。我碰到过好多次：我以为我要的是A，告诉AI的也是A，但做完之后才发现我真正需要的是B。所以我的做法是让AI先不急着干活，而是主动问我问题、探索上下文、把它的假设说出来让我确认。五个具体行为：扫描上下文、把假设显式化、挑战我说的话、看看行业里别人怎么做的、用小结果来试探方向。意图触达解决的是"规格要写什么"，其他组件解决的是"怎么写"。 Before writing specs, there's a prerequisite — figuring out what you actually want. Often, what you say you need isn't what you truly need. I've experienced this many times: I thought I wanted A, told AI to do A, but after it was done I realized I actually needed B. So my approach is to have AI hold off on executing and instead actively ask questions, explore context, and state its assumptions for me to confirm. Five specific behaviors: scan context, make assumptions explicit, challenge what I say, look at what others in the industry have done, and use small results to probe direction. Intent reaching solves "what should specs capture"; other components solve "how to write them."
§ 2.5
活标准双环审查 Living Standard Dual-Loop Review 已审查 ✓ Reviewed ✓
用"卡壳点"驱动标准进化——无法评分的维度就是盲点。本站6份指南经9轮审查。 "Stuck points" drive standard evolution — dimensions you can't score reveal blind spots. This site's 6 guides went through 9 review rounds.
双环机制：内环审查案例（AI输出），外环审查标准本身。用"卡壳点"驱动标准进化——在某个维度上无法评分时，说明标准有盲点。A类卡壳=标准缺失，B类卡壳=案例缺陷。本站的设计规范和IntentBridge自身的6份指南，都经过了9轮这样的审查。 A dual-loop mechanism: the inner loop reviews the case (AI output), the outer loop reviews the standard itself. "Stuck points" drive standard evolution — when you can't score on a dimension, it reveals a blind spot. A-type stuck = standard gap, B-type stuck = case defect. This site's design spec and IntentBridge's own 6 guides have all been through 9 rounds of this review.

§ 2.7
人类永远要做的事 What Humans Will Always Need to Do
不管AI多强，有些事只能人来做。不是技术不行，是事情本身的性质决定的。 No matter how powerful AI gets, some things only humans can do. It's not about technology falling short — it's the nature of the work itself.
这是我用AI做了这么多项目、烧了几十亿tokens之后的一个判断——不管AI进化到什么程度，有四件事永远需要人来做：把你的领域知识喂给AI（AI可能懂通用的地铁知识，但业务细节和具体业务需求场景不可能自己知道）、搞清楚自己到底想要什么（包括你自己还没意识到的部分）、检查AI的理解和你的意图之间还有没有差距、以及最终为结果负责。这不是技术不行的问题，是这些事本身的性质决定的。 This is a judgment I've formed after all these AI projects and burning through billions of tokens — no matter how much AI evolves, four things will always need humans: feeding your domain knowledge to AI (AI may know general metro concepts, but business details and specific business scenarios are impossible to know on its own), figuring out what you actually want (including what you haven't realized yourself), checking whether there's still a gap between AI's understanding and your intent, and ultimately taking responsibility for the result. This isn't about technology falling short — it's the nature of these tasks.
