IntentBridge 不是 Prompt Engineering 的变体，也不是某个工具或框架。它是人机协同的一种思想——核心命题是人的意图如何完整、可靠地转化为AI的执行。这个想法来源于我三年深度使用AI解决真实业务问题的过程中，反复遇到的一个痛点。 IntentBridge is not a variant of Prompt Engineering, nor is it a tool or framework. It is a philosophy of human-AI collaboration — the core proposition is how human intent can be completely and reliably translated into AI execution. This idea emerged from three years of deep AI usage solving real business problems, where I kept running into the same pain point.

§ 1

起因 — 真实痛点 Origin — Real pain points

2023年，我开始深度使用AI做数据分析、平台开发、产品设计。很快我发现一个反复出现的问题——每次和AI协作，结果和我的预期之间总有偏差。不是AI不够强，也不是我不会写提示词。问题出在更根本的地方。 In 2023, I started using AI intensively for data analysis, platform development, and product design. I soon noticed a recurring problem — every time I collaborated with AI, there was always a gap between the result and my expectation. It wasn't that AI wasn't powerful enough, nor that I couldn't write prompts. The problem was more fundamental.

我心里想的是A，告诉AI的是A′，AI理解的是A″，最终执行的是A′″′ What I have in mind is A, what I tell AI is A′, what AI understands is A″, what it finally executes is A′″′
意图在传递过程中逐级失真——这就是"意图-执行差距" Intent gets distorted at each step of translation — this is the "Intent-Execution Gap"

这四层偏移，在简单任务里不明显。但当你用AI做复杂的事情——搭平台、写规范、做决策支持——每一层偏移都会被放大，最终结果离你的真实意图越来越远。 These four layers of drift are barely noticeable in simple tasks. But when you use AI for complex work — building platforms, writing specs, supporting decisions — each layer of drift gets amplified, and the final result drifts further and further from your true intent.

所以这不是提示词的问题。提示词工程解决的是"怎么说"，而IntentBridge要解决的是更根本的问题——人机之间如何建立可靠的意图传递机制。 So this is not a prompt problem. Prompt engineering solves "how to say it," while IntentBridge addresses a more fundamental question — how to build a reliable intent transfer mechanism between humans and AI.

§ 2

框架 — 核心组成 Framework — Core Components

§ 2.1
核心架构：两层规格 Core Architecture: Two-Layer Specs 已审查 ✓ Reviewed ✓
AI的执行质量上限，由规格质量决定——协作规格+知识规格，缺一不可。 AI's execution quality ceiling is determined by spec quality — collaboration specs + knowledge specs, both indispensable.
AI的执行质量上限，由你给它的规格质量决定。规格分为两层，缺一不可：协作规格告诉AI怎么工作——工作方式、边界、约束（如CLAUDE.md）；知识规格告诉AI判断所需的领域依据——术语定义、规则集、案例库。只有协作规格没有知识规格，AI不知道依据什么做判断；只有知识规格没有协作规格，AI不知道怎么使用这些知识。这个洞察来源于AFC项目的实践。 AI's execution quality ceiling is determined by the quality of specs you give it. Specs have two layers, both indispensable: collaboration specs tell AI how to work — methods, boundaries, constraints (e.g., CLAUDE.md); knowledge specs provide the domain basis for AI's judgments — terminology, rule sets, case libraries. Collaboration specs without knowledge specs leave AI without a basis for judgment; knowledge specs without collaboration specs leave AI unsure how to use that knowledge. This insight came from the AFC project.
§ 2.2
协作规格层：六维评估 + 撰写指南 Collaboration Spec Layer: Six-Dim Assessment + Writing Guide 已审查 ✓ Reviewed ✓
六个维度评估协作规格质量（0-12分），配套撰写指南覆盖13项必填内容、7个反模式。 Six dimensions assess collaboration spec quality (0-12 points), with a complete writing guide covering 13 required items and 7 anti-patterns.
六维质量评估框架从六个维度评估协作规格的质量：冷启动充分性（AI能否仅凭文档独立上手）、执行成本控制、规则冲突检测、时间衰减保护、范围边界清晰度、内容正确性。每个维度0-2分，总分12分。基于六维框架的完整撰写指南（v1.4）覆盖13项必填内容、4步撰写流程、7个反模式，已适配 Claude Code、Cursor、Copilot、Windsurf 等主流AI编程工具。 The Six-Dimension Quality Assessment Framework evaluates collaboration spec quality across: cold start sufficiency (can AI orient itself from this doc alone?), execution cost control, rule conflict detection, temporal decay protection, scope boundary clarity, and content correctness. Each dimension scores 0–2, total 12. A complete writing guide (v1.4) based on this framework covers 13 required content items, a 4-step writing process, and 7 anti-patterns, adapted for Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot, Windsurf, and other major AI coding tools.
§ 2.3
知识规格层：领域知识显性化 Knowledge Spec Layer: Making Domain Knowledge Explicit 已审查 ✓ Reviewed ✓
面向领域专家——把你脑子里的专业判断，变成AI能理解、能使用的东西。 For domain experts — turning the professional judgment in your head into something AI can understand and use.
面向领域专家（非技术人员）的方法。核心问题是：你脑子里的专业判断，怎么变成AI能用的东西？四种隐性知识提取技法——反例法（从"这是错的"反推规则）、追问法（层层追问判断依据）、对比法（找相似案例的关键差异）、边界法（找规则的例外和失效条件）。提取后的知识以四种形态整理：术语表、规则集（IF-THEN）、范例库（好案例/坏案例/边界案例）、权威来源清单。五维质量评估（完整性、准确性、无歧义性、时效性、隐性知识覆盖率，0-10分）确保知识规格达标。 A method for domain experts (non-technical). The core question: how do you turn the professional judgment in your head into something AI can use? Four tacit-knowledge extraction techniques — counter-example (infer rules from "this is wrong"), deep questioning (drill into judgment basis layer by layer), comparative (find the critical difference between similar cases), and boundary (find exceptions and failure conditions). Extracted knowledge is organized into four formats: glossary, rule sets (IF-THEN), example libraries (good/bad/boundary cases), and authoritative source lists. A five-dimension quality assessment (completeness, accuracy, ambiguity-free, timeliness, tacit-knowledge coverage, 0–10 points) ensures knowledge spec quality.
§ 2.4
前置过程：意图触达 Pre-Spec Process: Intent Reaching
规格撰写之前的前置步骤。陈述需求≠真实需求——AI主动探索帮你发现真正的意图。 The pre-spec step. Stated requirements ≠ real requirements — AI proactively explores to uncover your true intent.
规格撰写之前的前置步骤——发现真实意图。陈述需求≠真实需求：你心里想的是A，告诉AI的是A'，但A和A'之间可能有关键差距。双环模型：外环是传统模式（陈述需求→AI执行→结果，差距在执行中积累），内环是意图触达模式（AI主动探索上下文→显式化假设→人类确认→意图更新→再执行）。5个AI主动探索行为：上下文扫描、显式假设、挑战陈述、市场/行业扫描、结果探针。意图触达解决的是"规格要捕捉什么"，其他组件解决的是"怎么写"。 The pre-spec step — discovering real intent. Stated requirements ≠ real requirements: what you have in mind is A, what you tell AI is A', and there can be critical gaps between them. Dual-loop model: the outer loop is traditional (state requirement → AI executes → result, gaps accumulate during execution); the inner loop is intent-reaching mode (AI proactively explores context → makes assumptions explicit → human confirms → intent updates → then executes). Five proactive AI behaviors: context scanning, explicit assumptions, challenging statements, market/industry scanning, result probing. Intent reaching solves "what should specs capture"; other components solve "how to write them."
§ 2.5
活标准双环审查 Living Standard Dual-Loop Review 已审查 ✓ Reviewed ✓
用"卡壳点"驱动标准进化——无法评分的维度就是盲点。本站6份指南经9轮审查。 "Stuck points" drive standard evolution — dimensions you can't score reveal blind spots. This site's 6 guides went through 9 review rounds.
双环机制：内环审查案例（AI输出），外环审查标准本身。用"卡壳点"驱动标准进化——在某个维度上无法评分时，说明标准有盲点。A类卡壳=标准缺失，B类卡壳=案例缺陷。本站的设计规范和IntentBridge自身的6份指南，都经过了9轮这样的审查。 A dual-loop mechanism: the inner loop reviews the case (AI output), the outer loop reviews the standard itself. "Stuck points" drive standard evolution — when you can't score on a dimension, it reveals a blind spot. A-type stuck = standard gap, B-type stuck = case defect. This site's design spec and IntentBridge's own 6 guides have all been through 9 rounds of this review.
§ 2.6
人机分工模型 Human-AI Division of Labor Model 已审查 ✓ Reviewed ✓
按意图清晰度和后果严重性划分——比"人管方向、AI管执行"精细得多。 Divided by intent clarity and consequence severity — far more nuanced than "humans steer, AI executes."
明确哪些决策必须由人做，哪些可以交给AI。按意图清晰度和后果严重性两个维度来划分，比简单说"人管方向、AI管执行"要精细。 Clarifies which decisions must be made by humans and which can be delegated to AI. Divided along two dimensions — intent clarity and consequence severity — more nuanced than simply saying "humans steer, AI executes."
§ 2.7
人类永久工作理论 Theory of Permanent Human Work
无论AI如何进化，有四类工作永远需要人类参与。这是事情本身的性质决定的。 No matter how AI evolves, four categories of work will always require humans. It's the nature of the work itself.
无论AI能力如何进化，有四类工作永远需要人类参与：领域知识输入、真实意图发现（包括你自己尚未意识到的部分）、意图与规格之间的差距识别、最终责任承担。这不是技术不够，是这件事本身的性质决定的。 No matter how AI evolves, four categories of work will always require humans: domain knowledge input, real intent discovery (including what you haven't yet realized yourself), intent-spec gap identification, and final responsibility bearing. This isn't about technology falling short — it's the nature of the work itself.
