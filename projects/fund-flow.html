§ 1

背景 Background

天池竞赛赛题——预测余额宝（天弘基金）未来每日的资金申购和赎回量。资金流受工资日、节假日、收益确认规则等多重周期叠加影响。 Tianchi Competition challenge — predict daily purchase and redemption volumes for Yu'e Bao (Tianhong Fund). Fund flows are influenced by overlapping cycles: payday, holidays, yield confirmation rules.

方案 Solution

构建双周期因子模型，分离月度周期和周度周期的叠加效应。结合 Prophet 进行趋势分解，手动编码收益确认延迟等业务规则特征。 Built a dual-cycle factor model to separate overlapping monthly and weekly cycle effects. Combined with Prophet for trend decomposition, manually encoding business-rule features like yield confirmation delays.
