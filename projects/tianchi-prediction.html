§ 1

背景 Background

阿里天池大数据竞赛——二手车价格预测赛题。数据来自某交易平台二手车交易记录，总数据量超过 40 万条，包含 31 列变量信息，其中 15 列为匿名变量。训练集 15 万条，测试集各 5 万条。这是一个典型的回归问题，要求根据品牌、车龄、里程等多维特征预测交易价格。33,814 支队伍参赛。 Alibaba Tianchi Big Data Competition — Used Car Price Prediction. Data sourced from a trading platform's used car records: 400k+ entries, 31 feature columns (15 anonymized). 150k training samples, 50k each for test sets A and B. A classic regression problem — predict transaction price from brand, age, mileage, and other features. 33,814 teams participated.

§ 2

方案 Solution

以特征工程为核心驱动力，结合 XGBoost 和 LightGBM 双模型集成。不依赖复杂模型架构，靠对数据的直觉理解构造关键特征。 Feature engineering as the core driver, combined with XGBoost and LightGBM dual-model ensemble. No complex architecture — relied on intuitive understanding of data to construct key features.

策略的核心是"先爆炸、后修剪"——先把31个原始特征疯狂扩展到163个，再用严格的筛选缩减到60个。为什么这样做？因为不知道哪些非线性变换（对数、平方根、立方、逆函数）在哪个价位段有效，干脆全试一遍，让数据自己说话。关键交互特征如"每车龄功率"（power_per_age）反映发动机相对质量，"每车龄里程"（km_per_age）反映使用强度——这些不是算法算出来的，是对二手车市场的直觉。 The core strategy was "explode first, prune later" — wildly expanding 31 raw features to 163, then rigorously filtering down to 60. Why? Because you don't know which nonlinear transformations (log, square root, cubic, inverse) work at which price range, so try them all and let the data decide. Key interaction features like "power per age" (engine quality relative to age) and "km per age" (usage intensity) came from market intuition, not algorithms.

特征筛选用了4种独立方法打分再加权融合：F-score（统计相关性）、互信息（信息论）、随机森林重要性（树模型视角）、Lasso系数（正则化视角）。权重分别为25%、25%、30%、20%。为什么不只用一种？因为单一方法各有盲区——统计学方法忽略非线性交互，树模型偏向高基数特征，正则化方法对共线特征不稳定。共识比个体更可靠。 Feature selection used 4 independent scoring methods with weighted fusion: F-score (statistical), mutual information (information-theoretic), random forest importance (tree-based), and Lasso coefficients (regularization). Weights: 25%, 25%, 30%, 20%. Why not just one? Each method has blind spots — statistical methods miss nonlinear interactions, tree models favor high-cardinality features, regularization is unstable with collinear features. Consensus beats individuals.

最终模型是5个XGBoost和LightGBM变体的加权集成。权重不是简单平均或逆误差加权，而是用SLSQP数学优化器在验证集上搜索全局最优——以狄利克雷分布随机初始化，运行10次取最优解，同时加入权重分散正则化来鼓励模型多样性。另一个关键教训：早期用testA做验证导致严重过拟合（MAE 6000+），切换到testB后才真正突破——数据泄漏是竞赛中最容易踩的坑。 The final model is a weighted ensemble of 5 XGBoost and LightGBM variants. Weights aren't simple averaging — they're mathematically optimized via SLSQP on the validation set, initialized with Dirichlet distribution, running 10 optimization rounds, plus a weight dispersion regularizer to encourage model diversity. Another critical lesson: early validation on testA caused severe overfitting (MAE 6000+), and the real breakthrough came after switching to testB — data leakage is the easiest trap in competitions.

§ 4

反思 Reflections

这个项目最大的认知转变是：特征工程远比模型架构重要。在有限的竞赛时间里，理解数据的业务含义——"每车龄功率"为什么有意义、品牌名称被混淆后怎么用目标编码绕过——比调参和堆叠模型有效得多。MAE从6000+到486的过程，不是靠换更复杂的模型，而是靠一次次更深入地理解数据。 The biggest insight from this project: feature engineering matters far more than model architecture. In limited competition time, understanding the business meaning behind data — why "power per age" makes sense, how target encoding works around anonymized brand names — is far more effective than tuning and stacking models. The journey from MAE 6000+ to 486 wasn't achieved by switching to more complex models, but by understanding the data more deeply at each iteration.

另一个教训是验证策略的重要性。testA的数据泄漏让我一度以为模型很强（验证集表现很好），实际提交后才发现是假象。5折分层交叉验证+正确的测试集选择，才给出了可靠的性能估计。这个经验在后来的余额宝资金流预测中同样适用。 Another lesson: validation strategy matters. Data leakage from testA made me think the model was strong (good validation scores), only to discover it was an illusion upon submission. 5-fold stratified cross-validation with the correct test set finally gave reliable performance estimates. This lesson carried directly into the Yu'e Bao fund flow prediction project that followed.
