§ 1

背景 Background

阿里天池大数据竞赛——二手车价格预测赛题。数据来自某交易平台二手车交易记录，总数据量超过 40 万条，包含 31 列变量信息，其中 15 列为匿名变量。训练集 15 万条，测试集各 5 万条。这是一个典型的回归问题，要求根据品牌、车龄、里程等多维特征预测交易价格。33,814 支队伍参赛。 Alibaba Tianchi Big Data Competition — Used Car Price Prediction. Data sourced from a trading platform's used car records: 400k+ entries, 31 feature columns (15 anonymized). 150k training samples, 50k each for test sets A and B. A classic regression problem — predict transaction price from brand, age, mileage, and other features. 33,814 teams participated.

§ 2

方案 Solution

用 XGBoost 和 LightGBM 做双模型集成，但重心放在特征工程上。没搞复杂模型架构，靠对数据的直觉理解构造关键特征。 Feature engineering as the core driver, combined with XGBoost and LightGBM dual-model ensemble. No complex architecture — relied on intuitive understanding of data to construct key features.

我的策略是"先爆炸、后修剪"——先把31个原始特征疯狂扩展到163个，再严格筛选缩减到60个。哪些非线性变换（对数、平方根、立方、逆函数）在哪个价位段有效，事先根本不知道，干脆全试一遍，让数据自己说话。"每车龄功率"（power_per_age）反映发动机相对质量，"每车龄里程"（km_per_age）反映使用强度——这些特征靠的是对二手车市场的直觉，算法算不出来。 My strategy: "explode first, prune later" — wildly expanding 31 raw features to 163, then rigorously filtering down to 60. You don't know which nonlinear transformations (log, square root, cubic, inverse) work at which price range, so just try them all and let the data decide. "Power per age" reflects engine quality relative to age, "km per age" reflects usage intensity — these came from used car market intuition, algorithms can't figure that out.

特征筛选用了4种独立方法打分再加权融合：F-score（统计相关性）、互信息（信息论）、随机森林重要性（树模型视角）、Lasso系数（正则化视角）。权重分别为25%、25%、30%、20%。只靠一种方法不行——每种各有盲区。统计学方法忽略非线性交互，树模型偏向高基数特征，正则化方法对共线特征不稳定。四种方法取共识，比任何一种单独用都靠谱。 Feature selection used 4 independent scoring methods with weighted fusion: F-score (statistical), mutual information (information-theoretic), random forest importance (tree-based), and Lasso coefficients (regularization). Weights: 25%, 25%, 30%, 20%. Relying on just one doesn't work — each has blind spots. Statistical methods miss nonlinear interactions, tree models favor high-cardinality features, regularization is unstable with collinear features. Four methods reaching consensus beats any single one.

最终模型是5个XGBoost和LightGBM变体的加权集成。权重用SLSQP数学优化器在验证集上搜索全局最优——以狄利克雷分布随机初始化，运行10次取最优解，同时加入权重分散正则化来鼓励模型多样性，没用简单平均。另一个关键教训：早期用testA做验证，本地MAE只有600左右，提交后却飙到6000+——因为testA测试集与训练集之间存在数据泄漏，验证分数虚高，完全不反映真实水平。换成testB做验证后才看到真实表现，并一路优化到486。数据泄漏最坑的地方：它让分数变好，让你在虚假的信号上自信地做优化。 The final model is a weighted ensemble of 5 XGBoost and LightGBM variants. Weights are mathematically optimized via SLSQP on the validation set — initialized with Dirichlet distribution, running 10 optimization rounds, plus a weight dispersion regularizer to encourage model diversity, no simple averaging. Another critical lesson: early validation on testA showed a local MAE around 600, but the submitted score spiked to 6000+ — testA had data leakage with the training set, inflating validation scores that didn't reflect real performance at all. Switching to testB revealed the true baseline and enabled the path to 486. The nastiest thing about data leakage: it makes your scores look better, letting you confidently optimize against a false signal.

§ 4

反思 Reflections

做完这个项目，我最大的感受是：特征工程比模型架构重要得多。在有限的竞赛时间里，理解数据的业务含义——"每车龄功率"为什么有意义、品牌名称被混淆后怎么用目标编码绕过——比调参和堆叠模型有效得多。MAE从6000+到486，一路靠的不是换更复杂的模型，是一次比一次更深入地理解数据。 After this project, my biggest takeaway: feature engineering matters far more than model architecture. In limited competition time, understanding the business meaning behind data — why "power per age" makes sense, how target encoding works around anonymized brand names — is far more effective than tuning and stacking models. The journey from MAE 6000+ to 486 wasn't about switching to more complex models — it was about understanding the data more deeply at each iteration.

另一个教训是验证策略的重要性。testA的数据泄漏让我一度以为模型很强（验证集表现很好），实际提交后才发现是假象。5折分层交叉验证+正确的测试集选择，才给出了可靠的性能估计。这个经验在后来的蚂蚁金服资金预测中同样适用。 Another lesson: validation strategy matters. Data leakage from testA made me think the model was strong (good validation scores), only to discover it was an illusion upon submission. 5-fold stratified cross-validation with the correct test set finally gave reliable performance estimates. This lesson carried directly into the Ant Financial fund prediction project that followed.
