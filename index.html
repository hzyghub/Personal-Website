AI协作理念AI Collaboration Philosophy

01
意图-执行差距Intent-Execution Gap

AI只理解你明确表达的意图，而非你真正的意图。这个差距是AI协作的根本问题。AI only understands what you explicitly express, not your true intent. This gap is the fundamental problem of AI collaboration.

02
规格工程Spec Engineering

系统化地编写AI能正确理解和执行的规范文档。不是写文档，是训练你的AI搭档。Systematically writing specifications that AI can correctly understand and execute. Not documentation — training your AI partner.

03
人机分工模型Human-AI Division of Labor

永久性人类工作（领域知识、真实意图发现、差距识别、责任归属）vs AI工作（格式化、审计、执行）Permanent human work (domain knowledge, true intent discovery, gap identification, accountability) vs AI work (formatting, auditing, execution)

04
活标准双环审查Living Standard Dual-Loop Review

用案例检验标准，用"卡壳点"反哺标准改进。A类卡壳修标准，B类卡壳修案例。Test standards with cases, feed "sticking points" back to improve standards. Type-A fixes the standard; Type-B fixes the case.

「好的规范不是写给人类看的说明书，而是让AI能独立行动的作战手册。」 "A good spec is not a manual for humans to read, but a field guide that enables AI to act independently."
