AFC数据智能分析平台AFC Data Intelligence Platform

西安地铁14号线机场段客流数据智能分析系统Xi'an Metro Line 14 Airport Section Passenger Flow Analysis System

FastAPI React DuckDB Text-to-SQL 多LLM适配Multi-LLM Adapter

西安地铁14号线日均6.27万笔交易来自四种格式各异的支付渠道（AFC、二维码、长安通、交通联合卡），长期无法统一分析，运营决策依赖经验判断。我设计了"离线导出→三阶段脱敏→DuckDB列式分析"的架构——选DuckDB而非PostgreSQL，因为列式存储在聚合查询上快5-10倍且零运维；解决最难的工程问题是二维码OD配对（二维码即时扣费无进出站关联，需通过脱敏用户hash+交易类型配对，成功率>80%）。前端采用演进式原子化架构（39个Widget独立自包含），AI模块通过工厂模式适配9家LLM厂商的OpenAI兼容接口，SSE流式响应实现逐字输出。MVP已用于领导汇报，推动运营管理从经验判断向数据驱动转型。 Xi'an Metro Line 14 generates 62.7K daily transactions across four payment channels (AFC, QR code, Chang'antong, transit card), each with different schemas, making unified analysis impossible. I designed an "offline export → 3-stage anonymization → DuckDB columnar analysis" architecture — choosing DuckDB over PostgreSQL for 5-10x faster aggregation queries with zero ops overhead. The hardest engineering challenge was QR code OD pairing (instant payment has no entry-exit link; I paired records via anonymized user hash + trade type with >80% success rate). The frontend uses an evolutionary atomic architecture (39 self-contained Widgets), and the AI module adapts 9 LLM providers via factory pattern with SSE streaming. MVP has been used in executive briefings, driving the shift from experience-based to data-driven operations.

二手车价格预测Used Car Price Prediction

阿里天池竞赛Alibaba Tianchi Competition

XGBoost LightGBM 特征工程Feature Eng. 模型融合Model Fusion
Top 1% （205 / 33,814）(205 / 33,814) MAE 486，从650优化，降幅25.2%, optimized from 650, 25.2% reduction

核心挑战在于15个匿名特征含义不明且极易过拟合（初期验证集MAE~600，提交后飙至6000+）。我构建了六层特征工程管线：对核心数值做多重数学变换（对数/平方根/多项式/排名），从注册日期深度挖掘车龄和周期性编码，对v系列匿名特征做聚合统计与内部交叉，构造功率×车龄×里程的三维交互，用贝叶斯平滑目标编码防止类别特征过拟合。从163个候选中通过4种方法（F检验+互信息+随机森林重要性+Lasso）加权融合评分精选60个。最终5模型集成（XGBoost×3 + LightGBM×2，不同深度和正则化确保多样性），用scipy SLSQP从10个随机初始点优化融合权重，MAE从650降至486。 The core challenge was 15 anonymous features with unclear meaning and severe overfitting risk (initial CV MAE ~600, submission score 6000+). I built a 6-layer feature engineering pipeline: multi-transform on key numerics (log/sqrt/polynomial/rank), deep car-age extraction with cyclic encoding, aggregate statistics and cross-products for anonymous v-features, 3-way interaction features (power×age×mileage), and Bayesian-smoothed target encoding. From 163 candidates, I selected 60 via 4-method fusion scoring (F-test + mutual info + RF importance + Lasso). Final 5-model ensemble (XGBoost×3 + LightGBM×2 with varied depth/regularization for diversity), weights optimized by scipy SLSQP from 10 random initial points, reducing MAE from 650 to 486.

余额宝资金流入流出预测Yu'E Bao Fund Flow Prediction

蚂蚁金服天池竞赛Ant Financial Tianchi Competition

双周期因子Dual-Cycle Factor XGBoost Prophet 特征工程Feature Eng.
竞赛得分Score 121.87

余额宝上亿用户每日资金流动的精确预测，关乎流动性风险管理。核心创新是双周期因子乘性分解模型——将资金流中的周内模式（weekday因子）和月内模式（day因子）独立提取后做乘法组合（prediction = base_level × weekday_factor × day_factor），比传统单维度时间特征更好地捕捉周期叠加。在构建day因子时发现了"10号效应"：每月10日申购因子高达1.248（比月均高25%），与发薪日资金集中流入高度相关。此外将余额宝收益确认延迟规则（T+2至T+4天不等）显式编码为特征，发现申购与2天前收益的相关性达0.75——业务理解比算法复杂度更重要。 Precisely predicting Yu'E Bao's daily fund flows for 100M+ users, critical for liquidity risk management. The core innovation is a dual-cycle factor multiplicative decomposition model — independently extracting weekday patterns and day-of-month patterns, then combining them multiplicatively (prediction = base_level × weekday_factor × day_factor), capturing overlapping cycles better than single-dimension temporal features. While building day factors, I discovered the "10th-day effect": the 10th of each month shows a purchase factor of 1.248 (25% above monthly average), strongly correlated with payday fund inflows. I also encoded Yu'E Bao's yield confirmation delay rules (T+2 to T+4) as explicit features, finding purchase-to-2-day-prior-yield correlation of 0.75 — business understanding matters more than algorithm complexity.

规格工程方法论 — 让AI从"猜你想做什么"变成"知道你要做什么"Spec Engineering Methodology — Making AI go from "guessing what you want" to "knowing what you need"

方法论Methodology 规格工程Spec Eng. 人机协作Human-AI Collab 活标准审查Living Standard Review

AI只理解你明确说出的意图，不理解你真正想要的——这就是意图-执行差距。我在开发AFC平台时发现，写给AI的规范文档和写给人的完全不同：AI每次会话从零开始、不会主动追问、面对矛盾规则会随机选择。为此我提炼出规格工程方法论，定义了六维质量框架（其中"冷启动充分性"和"执行成本控制"是AI协作场景特有的维度），通过8轮活标准双环审查持续改进——用测试案例的"卡壳点"反哺标准盲点，A类卡壳修标准、B类卡壳修案例。在AFC项目CLAUDE.md上验证：冷启动定向成功率从约40%提升至约95%，token消耗降低71%，总分从5.3升至10.8（+104%）。 AI only understands what you explicitly state, not what you truly want — this is the intent-execution gap. While building the AFC platform, I discovered that specifications written for AI differ fundamentally from those for humans: AI starts each session from scratch, never asks clarifying questions, and randomly chooses between conflicting rules. I distilled a Spec Engineering methodology, defining a six-dimensional quality framework (where "cold-start sufficiency" and "execution cost control" are dimensions unique to AI collaboration), continuously improved through 8 rounds of living standard dual-loop review — using test case "sticking points" to feed back into standard blindspots. Validated on the AFC project's CLAUDE.md: cold-start directional accuracy rose from ~40% to ~95%, token consumption dropped 71%, and quality score improved from 5.3 to 10.8 (+104%).

AI协作理念AI Collaboration Philosophy

01
意图-执行差距Intent-Execution Gap

AI不知道你想要但没说出口的偏好、组织约束和领域直觉。每次会话从零开始，只能靠猜测填补信息缺口。AI doesn't know your unspoken preferences, organizational constraints, or domain intuition. Each session starts from scratch, filling gaps with guesses.

02
规格工程Spec Engineering

两层文档体系缺一不可：协作规格告诉AI"怎么干"（员工手册），知识规格告诉AI"依据什么判断"（业务培训教材）。规格的机械写作AI能做，不可替代的是人类确认"是否捕捉了我的真实意图"。Two layers, both essential: collaboration specs tell AI "how to work" (employee handbook), knowledge specs tell AI "what to base judgments on" (training materials). AI can mechanically write specs; the irreplaceable human role is confirming "does this capture my true intent?"

03
人机分工模型Human-AI Division of Labor

人类四项永久工作：领域知识输入、真实意图发现（包括自己没意识到的那部分）、意图-规格差距识别、最终责任承担。随AI增强，对话轮数减少，但"这是我真正想要的吗"的验证永远需要人。Four permanent human tasks: domain knowledge input, true intent discovery (including what you haven't realized), intent-spec gap identification, and final accountability. As AI strengthens, conversation rounds decrease, but "is this what I really want?" always requires a human.

04
活标准双环审查Living Standard Dual-Loop Review

用测试案例的"卡壳点"反哺标准盲点。A类卡壳（打不了分）说明标准有漏洞，修标准；B类卡壳（明显扣分）说明案例有缺陷，修案例。8轮审查发现29个标准盲点全部修复。Use test case "sticking points" to reveal standard blindspots. Type-A (can't score) means the standard has gaps — fix the standard. Type-B (clear deduction) means the case is deficient — fix the case. 8 rounds found 29 standard blindspots, all fixed.
