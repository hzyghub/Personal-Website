§ 2

我走过的路 The Path I've Walked

2013 — 2017
长安大学，自动化专业。对科技和数学的敏感，从这里开始。 Chang'an University, Automation major. Where my sensitivity to technology and math began.
2018 — 2026
西安地铁，8年运营一线。每天看着海量数据沉睡在数据库里，没有人去理解它——业务分析全靠手动导出Excel。我开始想：这些数据的价值，不应该被浪费。 Xi'an Metro, 8 years on the operations frontline. Watching massive data sit dormant in databases every day with no one making sense of it — business analysis relied entirely on manual Excel exports. I started thinking: this data's value shouldn't go to waste.
2023 初 Early 2023
遇到 ChatGPT。两个问题同时浮现：数据到决策之间缺一个智能层，人和AI之间也缺一个可靠的意图传递机制。此后系统自学AI——课程、论文、实践并行。 Encountered ChatGPT. Two questions surfaced simultaneously: an intelligence layer is missing between data and decisions, and a reliable intent transfer mechanism is missing between humans and AI. Systematically self-taught AI — courses, papers, hands-on practice in parallel.
2025
天池数据竞赛 Top 1%（205/33,814），验证数据分析能力。与AI深度协作中切身体会到：AI不懂你的领域，你喂给它的领域知识质量，直接决定它执行的准确度。 Tianchi Data Competition Top 1% (205/33,814), validated data analysis skills. Deep AI collaboration made it clear: AI doesn't understand your domain — the quality of domain knowledge you feed it directly determines execution accuracy.
2025 — 2026
把8年地铁领域知识和AI结合，为14号线打造数据分析+AI智能体平台（MVP已验证）。多源数据融合，AI从Text-to-SQL起步，目标是Level 4自主推理智能体。前期花大量时间创建的业务文档和领域知识，才是AI能正确执行的前提。 Combined 8 years of metro domain expertise with AI to build a data analysis + AI agent platform for Line 14 (MVP verified). Multi-source data fusion, AI evolving from Text-to-SQL toward a Level 4 autonomous reasoning agent. The extensive upfront work creating business documents and domain knowledge was the real prerequisite for AI to execute correctly.
现在 Now
从AFC项目中提炼出 IntentBridge——协作规格告诉AI怎么工作，知识规格告诉AI判断所需的领域依据。两层缺一不可。方法论、产品、商业，逐步成型。 Distilled IntentBridge from the AFC project — collaboration specs tell AI how to work, knowledge specs provide the domain basis for AI's judgments. Both layers are indispensable. Methodology, product, business, taking shape progressively.
